Privacy Policy

Ouri Clips Poster is a personal tool run by Ouri (streamgrind.com) to post clips of his own livestreams to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts. Last updated: November 7, 2026.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the app connects an account to it. It is not offered to the public and no one else is asked to log in.

What it accesses

When the owner connects his TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (account ID). It uses the token only to upload and post videos that the owner's own automation created from his own streams.

What it stores

The access token is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub repository. No data is collected about TikTok viewers or other users, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and by deleting the stored token.
